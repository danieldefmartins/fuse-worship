# Fuse Worship — Brand brief (draft v1, 2026-10-03)

## Who we are
Fuse Worship is a worship ministry led by Pastor Daniel Martins. The songs are made with AI (Suno), and the band says so openly. Every song is sung in **English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese**.

**Meaning of "Fuse":**
- *ignite*: the spark of the Holy Spirit, fire on the altar;
- *unite*: one Body, three languages, kids and adults together.

## Lines
| Line | Sound | Audience | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fuse Worship** | slow worship + energetic worship | youth and adults | reverent, powerful, warm |
| **Fuse Kids** | upbeat modern kids pop | kids, parents, churches | joyful, bright, safe |

## Logo requirements
- A **mark (icon) + wordmark** "FUSE WORSHIP".
- The icon alone must read clearly at **avatar size** (Spotify, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok: a circle crop at 100 px).
- Versions: full colour, all white (for dark backgrounds), all black.
- **Fuse Kids** = the same icon in a rounder, more playful version, with brighter colours, so the two clearly belong together.
- Not allowed: clichés like a dove, a generic globe or praying hands; imitating any existing church's or band's logo; fine detail that disappears when small.
- Ideas to explore: a flame or spark that forms a cross; a lit fuse becoming a flame; three strands joining into one flame (the three languages).

## Colours (starting point)
- **Fuse Worship:** deep ink #0E0E14, ember orange #FF5A1F, gold #FFB547, warm white #FFF8F0.
- **Fuse Kids:** ember orange #FF6B2C, sunshine #FFC93C, sky #3CB4FF, white.

## Type
- Bold, modern, geometric sans for the wordmark, in all caps.
- Kids line: a rounded, friendly sans.

## Rules
- Always disclose AI where a platform asks.
- Never imitate real artists in names, voices or visuals.
