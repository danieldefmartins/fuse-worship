# Logo prompt for ChatGPT (round 1)

Design a logo for **FUSE WORSHIP**, a Christian worship ministry that sings in English, Spanish and Portuguese. "Fuse" means both *ignite* (the fire of the Holy Spirit) and *unite* (one Body, many languages).

Create 4 different concepts, each shown as icon + wordmark on a plain background:
1. A flame whose negative space forms a simple cross.
2. A lit fuse line that rises and turns into a flame.
3. Three strands that braid into one flame (three languages, one faith).
4. A bold geometric monogram "F" combined with a spark or flame.

Requirements:
- Flat vector style, bold and simple. It must still read when the icon alone is shrunk to a 100 px circle (Spotify/YouTube/Instagram avatar).
- Wordmark "FUSE WORSHIP" in a bold modern geometric sans, all caps, spelled exactly like that.
- Colours: deep ink #0E0E14, ember orange #FF5A1F, gold #FFB547, warm white #FFF8F0.
- No doves, globes or praying hands; no gradients-heavy 3D, no tiny details; don't resemble any existing church or band logo.

After I pick one, I'll ask for: a white version, a black version, the icon alone (square, centred) and a **FUSE KIDS** version (same icon, rounder and more playful, colours #FF6B2C, #FFC93C, #3CB4FF).
